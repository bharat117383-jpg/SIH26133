Rural Healthcare Mission

Distance should never decide your diagnosis.

Over 65% of India's population resides in rural regions. Swastya Sethu bridges the gap by linking village health workers, teleconsultation kiosks, and urban medical specialists into one unified digital lifeline.

  • Teleconsultation Kiosks

    Village health centres equipped for real-time video consultations with specialists.

  • Community Health Workers

    Trained local health ambassadors who bridge language, digital comfort, and physical care.

  • Mobile Diagnostic Units

    Mobile clinics delivering routine screening, pathology collection, and preventative care.
